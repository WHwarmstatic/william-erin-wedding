Getting here

Travel & Stay

Flying in

Richmond International Airport (RIC) is the closest airport. From there it’s about 25–35 minutes to Short Pump depending on traffic.

Driving

The celebration is planned for the Richmond / Short Pump area. Parking notes will be posted once the venue is confirmed.

Where to stay

We recommend booking in Short Pump or the West End for easy access. A hotel block will be listed here when it opens. Nearby options typically include properties around Short Pump Town Center.

Getting around

Rideshare is reliable in this part of Richmond. We’ll share shuttle details if the venue is better reached that way.
